## AI Record 08 — AI-Assisted Narration Script and Video Assembly

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| *Date* | 6–7 November 2026 |
| *Member who used the AI tool* | Eizel Mthana Conqueror 康哲 |
| *Tool* | Claude (Anthropic); FFmpeg and faster-whisper running on Eizel’s PC |
| *Purpose* | To turn the corrected script into words to read aloud, then assemble the team’s recordings into the seven videos, with captions and a tutorial guide. |

*Important prompt*

> Okay claude, this is all the prereocorded voiceover of all the members. We follow all the spoken script that you have created, so there is no miss. For every video, input the 10 second intro that we have made, and make any necessary changes according to you so that the prerecorded voiceover follows the timestamp of your script.

*Relevant output summary*

Seven MP4 videos using the team’s own voices, with the intro, slides from PPT V1 and further slides in the same design, captions, pause countdowns, original music and sound effects, and a guide. No synthetic voice was used. Later instructions added: “for the captions, just follow the voice, do not follow the script”, and “add sound effects to it and chill music background that does not dominate the sound of our voiceover”. Eizel then heard a fault: “the video still has audio glitching of the voiceover, remake it so that the voice is clear and not glitching and has the perfect timing to the subtitles”. Claude measured the damage in its own processing (merged microphone channels, time-stretching, cuts into the voice) and rebuilt all seven videos with one microphone channel per speaker, no stretching, denoising or compression, cuts in silence, and subtitles timed to the spoken words. Eizel then asked: “Identify what is causing the videos to have cutted audios in them. The cutted parts need to be fixed.” Claude found a noise gate in Eizel’s recordings, digital silence left between sentences by its own editing, and two recordings cut off at their start. It filled the silence with a continuous background matched to each recording’s hiss, and limited edits to true silence.

*Verification and team judgment*

- How it was checked: Claude measured the final MP4 files. Speech recognition heard 623 of 630 sentences in place, sudden jumps in the background sound fell from 637 to none, and 2 of 631 subtitles appear more than a quarter of a second before the voice. Every section starts on its script time, and the music is about 17 dB below the voice. Louis, Eizel and Ritchard then watched the rebuilt videos (Entry 06).
- Accepted: The script timing, which Eizel chose to keep, and captions that follow the voice.
- Revised or rejected: An earlier idea of AI-copied voices was cancelled by Eizel; the team recorded its own narration. In 47 sentences the recognition result looked like a mishearing, so the caption kept the script’s words.

*Outcome and learning*

The team has finished videos below the 250 MB limit. We learned to check recordings against the script before editing, because it found missing sections straight away, and that a person listening can catch what loudness and recognition measurements miss.
